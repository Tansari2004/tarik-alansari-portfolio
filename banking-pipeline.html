About the transaction data +

Each row is a simulated payment from one account to another, with a timestamp, banks, amounts, currencies, and payment method. The dataset includes transfers, purchases, card payments, and cheques.

IBM’s synthetic AML data contains generated records, not real customer transactions or anonymised bank records. The pipeline verifies that accepted records are accounted for and amounts reconcile across all 15 currencies.

Data & limitations

IBM synthetic payment data. No real customer records. Reports are exported snapshots of verified data.
