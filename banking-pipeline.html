WHY IT MATTERS

Good reports start
with trustworthy data.

A report is only as reliable as its data. This pipeline checks that every accepted transaction is accounted for and that amounts match across all 15 currencies before the results are published.

WHAT I BUILT

Three steps. One reliable pipeline.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Load transactions with Python, preserve the originals, and flag data issues.

  2. 02

    Check

    Organize the data in PostgreSQL and verify counts and currency amounts.

  3. 03

    Share

    Coordinate the workflow with Dagster, test with dbt, and report in Power BI.
