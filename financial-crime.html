WHY IT MATTERS

Help investigators focus
on the right cases first.

Positive labels make up only a tiny share of the synthetic data. Instead of relying on accuracy, this project measures how many labelled cases appear near the top of a limited review queue.

WHAT I BUILT

From transaction history to a clear review queue.

  1. 01

    Build signals

    Use SQL to create transaction features from information available before each scoring date.

  2. 02

    Rank cases

    Train on earlier dates, test on later dates, and assign every transaction a review score.

  3. 03

    Explain results

    Show ranked cases, model reasons, amounts, currencies, and transaction details in Power BI.

How to read the result

Among the top 1,000 later-period cases, 199 of 1,611 positive synthetic labels were surfaced. That is 19.9% precision and 12.35% recall at that review capacity.

The data and labels are synthetic. Review scores rank cases for human attention; they are not calibrated probabilities or proof of financial crime.
